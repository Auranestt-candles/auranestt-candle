CANDLE CARE

Care for your candle.
Enjoy the glow.

Trim the wick

Trim the wick to about 5 mm before each use.

First burn

Allow the wax to melt evenly to prevent tunneling.

Safety first

Never leave a burning candle unattended. Keep away from children, pets and flammable items.

Storage

Store in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight and extreme heat.

Best practice

Keep the candle on a stable, heat-safe surface and avoid short burns.

Enjoy mindfully

Light your candle, take a deep breath and let the soothing aroma elevate your space.
